Trinetra Secure Browser

Install the exam browser

Your exam runs inside Trinetra, a locked-down browser with camera proctoring. Install it before the exam day and check your camera.

Download for Windows Checking…

Windows may show “Windows protected your PC” because the installer is not code-signed yet. Choose More info → Run anyway.

Before your exam

  1. Run the installer. It installs for your user only — no administrator rights needed.
  2. Open Trinetra Secure Browser. Allow camera access when asked.
  3. If your proctor runs the exam from their laptop, click Change next to “Exam hosted on” and enter their address, e.g. 192.168.1.20:4100.
  4. Sign in with Google (optional — you stay signed in), enter the exam code, your name and roll number.
