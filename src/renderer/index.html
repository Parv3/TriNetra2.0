Exam rules and monitoring

This browser records what happens on your device while the exam is open. Read what is monitored, then confirm to continue.

Camera
Your webcam stays on. Software checks that one face is visible, that it is the person who signed in, and that you are looking at the screen. A frame is saved whenever something is flagged.
Browsing
You may open other tabs to look things up. Every address and search is recorded, searches must relate to the question on screen, and answer boxes are hidden from results. AI chat sites are blocked — use the built-in Guided help, which explains concepts but never gives answers.
Keyboard and mouse
Shortcut use (copy, paste, developer tools), typing rate and mouse activity are logged.
Your device
The exam runs full-screen. Switching to another application, connecting a second display, or running screen-sharing or remote-desktop software is reported.
Three warnings end the attempt. Each confirmed violation counts as one warning. On the last one your answers are submitted automatically and the attempt is sent for review.
