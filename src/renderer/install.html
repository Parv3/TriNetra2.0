What it checks

Everything a good invigilator watches, all at once.

Every check runs during the whole attempt and reports to the proctor with evidence, so a decision is never just a guess.

Camera proctoring

One face, the right face, eyes on the screen. A phone in view or a second person is flagged with a snapshot.

Locked-down browser

Full screen that can't be minimised, screen capture blocked, and second displays or remote-desktop tools reported.

Search that can't hand over answers

Tabs for looking things up, filtered to the question on screen. AI chat sites are blocked and answer boxes removed.

Guided help, never answers

A built-in tutor explains the idea behind a question so students can reason it out, without ever naming the option.

Live proctor console

Every candidate's camera, warnings as they happen, and a report with an integrity score for each attempt.

Exams on a schedule

Set when an exam opens and closes. Students see a countdown, and late starters only get the time that's left.

How it works

From download to first question in minutes.

Install once before exam day. After that, joining an exam takes an exam code and a camera check.

Install

Download and run the installer. If Windows shows “Windows protected your PC”, choose More info → Run anyway.

Join your exam

Enter the exam code from your proctor with your name and roll number, then confirm with your one-time code. For an online exam, set Exam hosted on to this site's address.

Camera check, then begin

Face the screen for one reference photo. The exam opens full-screen and your answers save as you go.

Privacy by design

Proctoring that stays on your laptop.

The camera is analysed on the student's own device. Nothing is streamed to a server unless the proctor opens a live view.

  • Only a snapshot is saved, and only when something is flagged.
  • Live video goes straight to your proctor, not stored anywhere.
  • You see exactly what is monitored and agree before the exam starts.

Questions

Windows says it “protected your PC”. Is the app safe?

Yes. That message appears for new apps that aren't code-signed yet. Click More info, then Run anyway. The installer only installs for your Windows user and doesn't need admin rights.

Is my video recorded?

No. The camera is analysed on your laptop. A single snapshot is saved only when something is flagged, so your proctor can review it. Live viewing goes directly to your proctor and isn't stored.

I'm on a Mac, a Chromebook or a phone.

The desktop app is for Windows. On other devices you can still take the exam in your browser with camera proctoring, if your proctor allows it. Full lockdown needs the Windows app.

What do I enter under “Exam hosted on”?

For an online exam, click Change and enter this site's address. If your proctor is running the exam from their own laptop, use the address they give you.

Can I leave the exam window?

The exam stays full-screen. Leaving it, minimising or switching apps is reported to your proctor. Use Submit exam when you're done.
